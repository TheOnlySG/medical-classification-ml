Clinical Input Guide

This guide explains the medical input parameters used in SymptoSense. Each table contains parameter descriptions along with commonly accepted healthy and unhealthy ranges to help users better understand the report forms.

Heart Disease Inputs

Cardiovascular metrics used for heart disease prediction and risk estimation.

Parameter Description Healthy Range Unhealthy / Risk Range
Age Age of the patient in years. Lower age with stable cardiovascular indicators Heart disease risk generally increases with age
Sex Biological sex of the patient used in cardiovascular risk analysis. No direct unhealthy value Certain heart disease risks differ statistically between sexes
Chest Pain Type (cp) Type of chest pain experienced by the patient. Typical or non-severe chest discomfort Severe or exercise-induced chest pain may indicate cardiovascular disease
Resting Blood Pressure (trestbps) Blood pressure measured while the patient is at rest. Around 90-120 mmHg systolic High resting blood pressure may indicate hypertension-related risk
Cholesterol (chol) Serum cholesterol level measured in mg/dL. Below 200 mg/dL High cholesterol increases cardiovascular disease risk
Fasting Blood Sugar (fbs) Blood sugar level measured after fasting. Below 120 mg/dL Elevated fasting blood sugar may indicate metabolic complications
Resting ECG (restecg) Results of the resting electrocardiographic test. Normal ECG readings Abnormal ECG patterns may indicate heart abnormalities
Maximum Heart Rate (thalach) Highest heart rate achieved during physical activity testing. Healthy exercise response Abnormally low maximum heart rate may indicate cardiovascular issues
Exercise Induced Angina (exang) Whether chest pain occurs during exercise. No exercise-induced chest pain Presence of angina during exercise may indicate reduced blood flow
Oldpeak ST depression caused by exercise relative to rest. Low ST depression values Higher values may indicate abnormal cardiac stress response
Slope Slope of the peak exercise ST segment. Normal upward slope patterns Flat or downward slopes may indicate heart disease
Number of Major Vessels (ca) Number of major blood vessels visible during fluoroscopy. Lower vessel obstruction count Higher vessel blockage count strongly correlates with heart disease
Thal Result of thalassemia-related cardiac stress testing. Normal blood flow patterns Reversible or fixed defects may indicate cardiovascular abnormalities

Tumor Analysis Inputs

Clinical tumor measurement features used in ML-based tumor classification.

Parameter Description Healthy Range Unhealthy / Risk Range
Radius Mean Average distance from the center of the tumor to its outer edge. Smaller and regular radius values Larger radius values may indicate abnormal tumor growth
Texture Mean Variation in gray-scale intensity within the tumor image. Smooth and uniform texture Irregular texture patterns may indicate malignancy
Perimeter Mean Average perimeter length of the tumor boundary. Smaller perimeter values Larger perimeter values may indicate tumor expansion
Area Mean Average area occupied by the tumor. Lower and stable area measurements Large tumor area may indicate higher clinical concern
Concavity Mean Measures how deeply indented the tumor boundary appears. Smooth outer boundaries Higher concavity may indicate irregular tumor shape
Concave Points Mean Number of concave portions detected on the tumor boundary. Fewer concave points Increased concave points are commonly associated with malignant tumors
Radius Worst Largest radius measurement observed in the tumor sample. Lower worst-case radius High worst-case radius is a strong malignancy indicator
Perimeter Worst Largest observed tumor perimeter measurement. Smaller perimeter values Larger perimeter values may indicate aggressive tumor growth
Area Worst Largest observed tumor area in the dataset sample. Smaller tumor area High area values are strongly associated with malignancy
Concave Points Worst Highest number of concave boundary points observed. Smooth and less fragmented boundary High concave point values strongly correlate with malignant tumors

Diabetes Inputs

Health indicators used for diabetes prediction and metabolic risk assessment.

Parameter Description Healthy Range Unhealthy / Risk Range
Pregnancies Number of times the patient has been pregnant. Lower pregnancy count with stable glucose levels High pregnancy count combined with abnormal glucose may increase diabetes risk
Glucose Plasma glucose concentration level in blood. Around 70–99 mg/dL while fasting High glucose levels are strongly associated with diabetes
Blood Pressure Diastolic blood pressure measurement. Around 80 mmHg Very high or very low blood pressure may indicate health complications
Skin Thickness Thickness of triceps skin fold used to estimate body fat. Moderate skin thickness values Higher values may correlate with obesity-related diabetes risk
Insulin Insulin concentration measured in blood. Balanced insulin response Extremely high or low insulin values may indicate metabolic abnormalities
BMI Body Mass Index calculated using weight and height. Between 18.5 and 24.9 BMI above 30 is commonly associated with obesity and diabetes risk
Diabetes Pedigree Function Score estimating hereditary diabetes likelihood based on family history. Lower hereditary risk score Higher values indicate stronger genetic predisposition to diabetes
Age Age of the patient in years. Lower age with stable metabolic indicators Diabetes risk generally increases with age
